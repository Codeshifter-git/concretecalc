How it works

How the concrete curb calculator works.

A curb and gutter pour is two boxes side by side. Work out each cross-section, add them, and multiply by the run.

The formulas you needCurb area = curb width × curb heightGutter area = gutter width × gutter thicknessVolume = length × (curb area + gutter area)All sizes are turned into feet first. One cubic yard = 27 cubic feet = 0.765 cubic meters.
01

Work out the curb cross-section

The curb is the upright box. Width times height, in feet. A 6 by 6 inch curb is 0.5 by 0.5 feet.

0.5 ft × 0.5 ft = 0.25 sq ft
02

Work out the gutter cross-section

The gutter is the flat strip next to the curb. Width times thickness, in feet. An 18-inch gutter at 6 inches thick is 1.5 by 0.5 feet.

1.5 ft × 0.5 ft = 0.75 sq ft
03

Add the two areas

Add the curb area and the gutter area to get the full cross-section.

0.25 sq ft + 0.75 sq ft = 1.00 sq ft
04

Multiply by the run length

Cross-section times length gives cubic feet. This is the net volume, before waste.

1.00 sq ft × 100 ft = 100 ft³
05

Divide by 27, then add waste

One cubic yard holds 27 cubic feet. Divide, then add 10% waste for spills and uneven forms.

100 ft³ ÷ 27 = 3.70 yd³ · × 1.10 = 4.07 yd³

Task 1: 80 ft of curb and gutter at the standard sizes. How many cubic yards? +

Work out the cubic yards for 80 feet of curb and gutter with a 6 by 6 inch curb and an 18-inch gutter at 6 inches thick, before waste.

Solution: Cross-section is 0.25 + 0.75 = 1.00 square foot. 1.00 × 80 = 80 cubic feet. 80 ÷ 27 = 2.96 cubic yards.

Task 2: 150 ft of curb and gutter, 6×6 in curb, 12 in gutter at 6 in thick. How many cubic yards? +

Work out the cubic yards for 150 feet of curb and gutter with a 6 by 6 inch curb and a 12-inch gutter at 6 inches thick, before waste.

Solution: Curb is 0.25 square feet and the gutter is 1.0 × 0.5 = 0.50 square feet, so the cross-section is 0.75 square feet. 0.75 × 150 = 112.50 cubic feet. 112.50 ÷ 27 = 4.17 cubic yards.

Still wondering?

Questions people ask.

What is curb and gutter? +

It is the concrete edge along a street. The curb is the upright edge that keeps cars off the sidewalk, and the gutter is the flat strip beside it that carries rain water to the drain. They are usually poured together as one piece.

What size is a standard curb and gutter? +

Sizes vary by city — each city sets its own standard in its street rules, so there is no single standard size. A 6 by 6 inch curb with an 18-inch gutter at 6 inches thick is a common size, and it is the default on this page. Check your city rules before you order.

How many bags of concrete do I need for curb and gutter? +

Divide the ordered cubic feet by the yield of one bag. An 80-lb bag fills 0.60 cubic feet. 100 feet of curb and gutter at the default sizes is 100 cubic feet before waste, which is 167 bags of 80-lb mix. The calculator shows all four bag sizes, with waste included.
