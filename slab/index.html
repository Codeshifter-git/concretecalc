How it works

How the concrete slab calculator works.

A slab is a flat box. Its volume is length times width times thickness — then divide by 27 to get cubic yards.

The formulas you needVolume = length × width × thicknessCubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27Ordered yards = net yards × (1 + waste)One cubic yard = 27 cubic feet = 0.765 cubic meters.
01

Measure length and width

Measure the slab in feet, where it will go. A patio that is 10 ft long and 10 ft wide covers 100 square feet.

10 ft × 10 ft = 100 sq ft
02

Turn thickness into feet

Thickness is often given in inches. Divide inches by 12 to get feet. 4 inches is 0.33 feet.

4 in ÷ 12 = 0.33 ft
03

Multiply to get cubic feet

Length times width times thickness gives cubic feet. This is the net volume, before waste.

10 ft × 10 ft × 0.33 ft = 33.33 ft³
04

Divide by 27 for cubic yards

One cubic yard holds 27 cubic feet, so divide the cubic feet by 27.

33.33 ft³ ÷ 27 = 1.23 yd³
05

Add waste, then order

Spills, uneven ground, and a little extra in the forms eat concrete. Add 10% for a clean job, 15% for rough ground.

1.23 yd³ × 1.10 = 1.36 yd³

Task 1: A patio is 12 ft by 15 ft and 4 in thick. How many cubic yards, before waste? +

A patio is 12 ft long, 15 ft wide, and 4 in thick. Work out the cubic yards, before any waste is added.

Solution: 4 in is 4 ÷ 12 = 0.33 ft. 12 ft × 15 ft × 0.33 ft = 60 cubic feet. 60 ÷ 27 = 2.22 cubic yards.

Task 2: How many 80-lb bags for the 10×10 ft slab at 4 in thick, with 10% waste? +

Take the 10 ft by 10 ft slab at 4 in thick, add 10% waste, then work out how many 80-lb bags that is. One 80-lb bag fills 0.60 cubic feet.

Solution: Net volume is 33.33 cubic feet. With 10% waste that is 36.67 cubic feet. 36.67 ÷ 0.60 = 61.11, so round up to 62 bags.

Questions people ask.

How much concrete do I need for a 10×10 slab? +

At 4 inches thick, a 10 ft by 10 ft slab needs 1.23 cubic yards, or 33.33 cubic feet. With 10% waste, order 1.36 cubic yards. At 6 inches thick, the same slab needs 1.85 cubic yards.

How thick should a concrete slab be? +

A 4-inch slab suits a patio or walkway. A driveway or a slab that holds a car usually needs 6 inches. When in doubt, ask a local contractor — the right thickness depends on the soil and the load.

How many bags of concrete do I need for a slab? +

Divide the ordered cubic feet by the yield of one bag. An 80-lb bag fills 0.60 cubic feet, a 60-lb bag fills 0.45, a 50-lb bag fills 0.375, and a 40-lb bag fills 0.30. For the 10×10 ft slab at 4 in thick with 10% waste, that is 36.67 cubic feet, or 62 bags of 80-lb mix. The calculator shows all four bag sizes.

How many cubic yards do I need for a driveway? +

It depends on the size and the thickness. A 12 ft by 20 ft driveway at 6 inches thick needs 4.44 cubic yards. Measure your own driveway, pick the thickness, and the calculator does the rest. For help picking a thickness, see the thickness guide.
