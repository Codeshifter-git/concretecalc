Concrete cost: how much per cubic yard?

Ready-mix concrete usually runs in the low hundreds of dollars per cubic yard, before delivery. The price changes by region, mix, and order size. Call two local plants for a real quote.

Priced by the cubic yard

Ready-mix plants quote by the cubic yard. There is no single national price: the region, the mix strength, the aggregate, and the delivery distance all move the number. That is why this guide gives a range, not a fixed price — the only exact number is the one a local plant gives you. Work out how many yards you need first with the concrete calculator, then call for quotes.

Short-load fees and minimums

A full mixer truck holds about 10 cubic yards. Order less than a full truck and many plants charge a short-load fee, because the truck still has to make the trip. Many plants also set a minimum order, often around 1 to 1.5 cubic yards. Small orders cost more per yard, so it pays to know the fee and the minimum before you order.

Order a little extra, not a little short

Running short mid-pour is worse than having a little left over: a second truck costs time and money, and the joint where the two pours meet is a weak spot. Add 5 to 10% for a clean pour, and 10 to 15% for rough or uneven ground. Round up to the next quarter or half yard if the plant sells that way.

Bags or a truck?

Around 1 cubic yard is the crossover point. Below that, bags are practical and you avoid delivery fees and minimums. Above that, mixing dozens of bags by hand takes hours, and a ready-mix truck is the easier choice. The bags guide shows the bag counts so you can compare both options for your pour.

How to get a real quote

Call two local plants and give each the same details: length, width, and thickness of the pour, the mix you need, and the delivery address. Ask what is included in the quote — delivery, the short-load fee, the minimum order, and any waiting time at the site. Two quotes for the same job tell you the real local price. The slab thickness guide helps you settle the thickness before you call, so the quote is for the right amount.

Questions people ask

How much does a cubic yard of concrete cost? +

Usually in the low hundreds of dollars per cubic yard, before delivery. Prices change by region and mix, so call a local plant for an exact number.

What is a short-load fee? +

A fee for ordering less than a full truckload, about 10 cubic yards. The plant still sends a truck, so small orders cost more per yard.

Is there a minimum order for ready-mix? +

Often yes — many plants set a minimum around 1 to 1.5 cubic yards. Ask when you call for a quote.

Is it cheaper to mix bags or order a truck? +

For small jobs under about 1 cubic yard, bags are practical. Above that, mixing dozens of bags takes hours, and a ready-mix truck is the easier choice. Compare both quotes before you decide.

What makes the price go up? +

A stronger mix, extra delivery distance, a short load, and a small order. Ask the plant what is included in the quote so nothing surprises you.
