Guide · Curing

How long does concrete take to cure?

Concrete takes 28 days to reach its full design strength. You can walk on it after 24 to 48 hours, and drive a light vehicle on it after 7 days. Curing is a chemical reaction with water, not just drying out.

Curing is not drying

Fresh concrete hardens because cement reacts with water. This reaction is called hydration, and it goes on for weeks. Drying out is different: if the water leaves too soon, the reaction stops and the surface stays weak and dusty. That is why curing is about keeping water in, not getting it out.

The curing timeline

Full design strength28 days

These are normal times for standard concrete in mild weather. Cold weather slows everything down; hot weather speeds it up. The 28-day mark is the standard engineers use when they talk about a mix reaching its design strength.

Keep it damp for the first week

The first week matters most, because that is when the concrete gains strength fastest. Mist the surface with water, or cover it with plastic sheeting or wet burlap, so sun and wind cannot dry it out. Do not let the surface dry and re-wet in cycles; steady dampness is the goal.

Cold and hot weather

Cold slows curing, and water inside fresh concrete can freeze, which damages it. In cold weather, cover the pour and keep it from freezing for the first few days. Heat speeds curing, which sounds good but leaves less time to finish the surface, and the top can dry too fast. In hot weather, pour in the morning or evening, keep the surface damp, and work fast. If you are planning the pour, check the slab thickness guide and the slab calculator first so the size and the order are settled before the truck arrives.

A simple plan for a new patio slab

Day 1: pour, finish, and cover the slab to hold in moisture. Day 2: walk on it lightly if you must, but keep heavy loads off. Day 7: light use is fine. Day 28: full strength — treat it as done.

Still wondering?

Questions people ask

How long before you can walk on concrete? +

After 24 to 48 hours. It is firm enough for foot traffic, but keep heavy loads off it.

How long before you can drive on concrete? +

Wait at least 7 days before a light vehicle. Heavy loads should wait until the concrete reaches full strength at 28 days.

How long does concrete take to fully cure? +

28 days to reach full design strength. It keeps gaining a little strength after that, but 28 days is the standard.

What happens if concrete dries too fast? +

The surface can crack and stay weak, because curing stops when the water is gone. Keep it damp for the first week.

Does rain help concrete cure? +

After the concrete has set, water helps curing. But heavy rain in the first few hours can wash the surface and weaken it, so cover a fresh pour if rain is coming.
