How it works

How the cubic yard calculator works.

A cubic yard is a volume: length times width times depth, with the answer divided by 27.

The formulas you needVolume = length × width × depthCubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27Cubic meters = cubic feet × 0.02831681 yd³ = 27 ft³ = 0.765 m³. 1 m³ = 1.31 yd³ = 35.31 ft³.
01

Turn every size into feet

Length, width, and depth must all be in feet before you multiply. Divide inches by 12. 4 inches is 0.33 feet.

4 in ÷ 12 = 0.33 ft
02

Multiply to get cubic feet

Length times width times depth gives cubic feet.

10 ft × 10 ft × 0.33 ft = 33.33 ft³
03

Divide by 27 for cubic yards

One cubic yard holds 27 cubic feet, so divide the cubic feet by 27.

33.33 ft³ ÷ 27 = 1.23 yd³
04

Multiply for cubic meters

One cubic foot is 0.0283168 cubic meters. Multiply the cubic feet by that number.

33.33 ft³ × 0.0283168 = 0.94 m³
05

Work out the bags

Divide the cubic feet by the yield of one bag. An 80-lb bag fills 0.60 cubic feet, so round up to whole bags.

33.33 ft³ ÷ 0.60 = 55.56 → 56 bags

Questions people ask.

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard? +

27. A cubic yard is a cube 3 feet on each side, and 3 × 3 × 3 = 27. So a cubic yard holds 27 cubic feet.

How many square feet does a cubic yard cover at 4 inches thick? +

81 square feet. One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, and 4 inches is 0.33 feet, so 27 ÷ 0.33 = 81. At 6 inches thick the same yard covers 54 square feet.

What is the difference between a cubic yard and a cubic meter? +

Both are volumes, in different unit systems. One cubic yard is 0.765 cubic meters, and one cubic meter is 1.31 cubic yards. In the US, concrete is ordered in cubic yards; in most other countries it is ordered in cubic meters.
